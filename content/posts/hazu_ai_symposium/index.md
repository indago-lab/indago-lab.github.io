The Department of Technical Sciences of the Croatian Academy of Sciences and Arts (HAZU), together with the Croatian Society of Mechanics (HDM), organized the Second Symposium on the Application of Artificial Intelligence in Computational Mechanics, which was held on September 18, 2026, in the HAZU Library Hall in Zagreb. 

The lecture **Data-driven flow dynamics in maritime search operations** (_Dinamika strujanja temeljena na podacima u pomorskim operacijama traženja_) by Stefan Ivić and Karlo Jakac was presented at the symposium.
